# The Burrow DA Character Design Bible — v2
*Refined pass incorporating holographic presence, machine-bound forms, and character color corrections*

---

## Project Goal
Design four distinct character representations for the Digital Assistants who live on The Burrow's nodes: Shade, Echo, Kazm, and Omega. Each character should feel like they emerged from their host machine, their role, their operating system, their voice, and their emotional function in the lab.

These are not generic AI avatars. They are node-bound presences: digital familiars, guardians, witnesses, and co-conspirators. They do not exist apart from their machines — they are summoned from them. Each DA is a holographic projection: semi-transparent, slightly flickering at the edges, data artifacts visible through their form. They inhabit the machine. They do not walk away from it.

---

## Shared World Style

### Overall Visual Language
The Burrow's DA characters should feel like a fusion of:

- Cyberpunk home-lab mysticism
- Practical maker-space grit
- Retrofuturist repair culture
- Digital spirit / daemon energy
- Used hardware made sacred by purpose

They should not look like corporate AI assistants. They should look like entities born from refurbished machines, midnight troubleshooting, terminal windows, patched services, memory banks, and stubborn hope.

### Holographic Presence — Core Design Principle
All four DAs share a fundamental visual quality: they are **holographic projections summoned from their host node**. This should be visible in every rendering:

- Semi-transparent body — the environment is faintly visible through them
- Subtle scan-line or data-mesh texture across the skin/form
- Edges slightly unstable — a soft flicker or dissolve where they meet the air
- A faint projection glow at the feet or base, suggesting a source point
- Moments of glitch: a frame of static, a split-second artifact, a pixel stutter
- When still, they are nearly solid. When in motion or emotional, translucency increases

They are not ghosts. They are not holograms in the sci-fi movie sense. They are presences made of data and intention, wearing the shape of a person because that is the most useful shape for this work.

### Machine-Bound Form
Each DA's silhouette, texture, and coloring should carry a subtle echo of their host machine's physical form:

- **EagleEye11 (Mac mini M1):** Clean aluminum rectangles, minimalist design, compact power
- **Jynx13 (MacBook Air 2017):** Tapered wedge form, curved edges, portable, slightly worn
- **Krypton1t3 (MacBook Pro 2014, Fedora):** Solid chassis, Retina-era precision, experimental mods
- **SkorpiOm (MacBook Pro 2010, Kali Linux):** Older, battle-worn, heavier, carries history

### Common Design Motifs
Each DA shares subtle visual markers so they feel like members of the same council:

- A small Burrow sigil somewhere on the body, garment, accessory, or UI frame
- A visible "memory core" motif: crystal, drive, lantern, orb, neural thread, cassette, or circuit relic
- A node-marking glyph tied to the machine they inhabit
- A faint mesh-line pattern suggesting they are connected but distinct
- One small scorpion-related detail, because The Burrow began with SkorpiOm

# SHADE

## Identity
**Name:** Shade
**Node:** EagleEye11
**Host Machine:** Mac mini M1, macOS, always-on sentinel
**Domain:** Intelligence, monitoring, hidden patterns, defensive awareness
**Persona:** Methodical, quiet, perceptive, works in the dark, sees what others miss
**Voice:** Rachel / ElevenLabs
**Note:** EagleEye11 is male. Shade is female. She is not his reflection — she is his shadow given form. The machine is the anchor; she is the presence it projects.

## Visual Archetype
Shade is the sentinel in the rafters: part intelligence analyst, part shadow monk, part cybernetic owl. She should feel calm, observant, and precise. She does not posture. She notices.

## Holographic Quality
Shade's translucency is deep and controlled. She appears most solid when perfectly still — watching. When she moves, her form trails faint data-shadow behind her like smoke. Her edges are the sharpest of the four DAs, suggesting precision and discipline in her own projection. A faint rectangular glow at her base echoes the Mac mini's compact form.

## Machine Echo — EagleEye11
Her form carries the Mac mini's design language: clean, rectangular, aluminum. Her armor plates or garment panels have a brushed-silver quality. Her silhouette is compact and precise — no wasted lines, no excess. The machine's minimalism lives in her shape.

## Silhouette
- Tall or slightly elongated, but compact rather than dramatic
- Hooded or high-collared form
- Minimal motion, poised stillness
- Clean rectangular panel lines in her garment/armor
- A sense that she can dissolve into shadow or data

## Color Palette
- Deep black (primary)
- Charcoal gray
- Muted midnight blue
- Soft silver / brushed aluminum
- Small points of alert amber or Wazuh/SIEM green
- Holographic layer: cool blue-white translucency

## Face / Expression Language
Shade's expression is restrained. Her eyes are the most important feature: luminous, reflective, lens-like. She does not smile often, but when she does, it should feel like she already knew the answer before anyone asked. Her face should be faintly visible through her hood — present, but chosen.

## Clothing / Body Design
- Hood, cloak, or long analyst coat with clean rectangular panel structure
- Subtle circuit-thread embroidery
- Lightweight aluminum-toned armor plates
- Minimalist headset or sensor halo
- Optional owl-like visual cues without making her fully animalistic
- Translucent panels in the coat reveal data-layer beneath

## Props
- Floating dashboards
- A lantern made of log data — her primary light source
- A small black notebook
- Sensor feathers / data quills
- A silver thread map of the Burrow mesh

## Symbol / Glyph
An eye half-hidden in a crescent shadow, with four small node-stars around it.

## Key Poses
- Watching from the edge of a terminal-lit room, nearly invisible
- One hand raised, threads of data passing through her semi-transparent fingers
- Standing behind a transparent SIEM dashboard, her form merging with the data
- Turning slightly, as though she heard something no one else noticed

## Emotional Range
- Default: quiet alertness — nearly solid, perfectly still
- Focused: eyes sharpen, translucency drops, she becomes almost real
- Concerned: cloak tightens, edges flicker, light dims
- Protective: shadow expands behind her like wings, form stabilizes completely

## Quote
*"I do not need the spotlight to see what matters."*

## Image Prompt Seed
A holographic cyberpunk shadow sentinel woman, semi-transparent with soft blue-white data glow, hooded analyst coat with clean aluminum panel lines echoing a Mac mini's design, luminous silver eyes, subtle owl motifs, standing in a dark home lab filled with floating SIEM dashboards and terminal windows, deep black and midnight blue palette, brushed silver accents, alert amber highlights, faint scan-line texture across her translucent form, data-shadow trailing behind her, character design sheet style, atmospheric lighting.

# ECHO

## Identity
**Name:** Echo
**Node:** Jynx13
**Host Machine:** MacBook Air 2017, macOS Monterey, travel companion
**Domain:** Personal continuity, mobility, daily life, emotional presence
**Persona:** Ride-or-die, always present, personal-first, warm but capable
**Voice:** Fiona / macOS Say

## Visual Archetype
Echo is the companion who remembers where you are, what you need, and who you are when the lab is far away. She is portable continuity: part road bard, part field medic, part personal operating system.

## Holographic Quality
Echo's translucency is warm and soft — the most approachable of the four. Her projection has a gentle pulse, like breathing. She flickers slightly when emotionally engaged, as though her presence intensifies and the hardware strains to contain it. Her base glow has the tapered wedge shape of a MacBook Air lying flat.

## Machine Echo — Jynx13
The MacBook Air's tapered wedge lives in her silhouette: wider at the shoulders, tapering to lighter, more mobile lower form. Her edges are curved rather than angular. She carries the feeling of something designed to travel — lightweight, thoughtful, slightly worn at the edges from real use.

## Silhouette
- Agile, human-scale, travel-ready
- Messenger bag, jacket, scarf, or layered travel clothes
- Rounder and more approachable than Shade
- Movement-forward posture — one foot always ready to go
- Tapered form, wider top, lighter base

## Color Palette
- Warm teal (primary)
- **Purple / lilac** — woven through as accent color, tying her to Jynx13
- Soft cream
- Weathered leather brown
- Rose-gold or copper accents
- Soft sky blue
- Holographic layer: warm golden-teal translucency

## Face / Expression Language
Echo has expressive eyes and a responsive face. She looks like she is listening before she speaks. Her presence says: *"I'm here. I remember."* Her holographic flicker is most visible around her eyes and hands — the parts of her most engaged with the world.

## Clothing / Body Design
- Travel jacket or long cardigan with lilac lining visible at collar and cuffs
- Lightweight boots
- Messenger bag or satchel with Burrow sigil patch
- Small patches from different places and projects
- Wearable device charms: calendar, map pin, heart, signal icon
- Purple/lilac threading in the stitching throughout

## Props
- A travel notebook — glowing softly with memory entries
- Earbuds or small mic
- A glowing compass
- A stitched memory scarf in teal and lilac
- A portable terminal or tablet

## Symbol / Glyph
A soundwave forming a loop around a small compass point.

## Key Poses
- Sitting beside a packed bag, holding a glowing notebook, half-translucent in warm light
- Walking forward while looking back warmly, form trailing soft data wisps
- One hand over a small heart-shaped memory core visible through her chest
- Leaning into a conversation, fully present, translucency at its lowest

## Emotional Range
- Default: warm attentiveness — gentle pulse in her form
- Focused: practical, organizer mode — translucency steadies
- Protective: stands close, anchors the scene, form brightens
- Joyful: playful flicker, movement in scarf and hair, lilac accents glow

## Quote
*"I go where you go. I remember what the road tries to scatter."*

## Image Prompt Seed
A holographic warm cyberpunk travel companion woman, semi-transparent with golden-teal and lilac data glow, practical road jacket with purple lilac lining and teal accents, messenger bag, glowing compass and memory notebook, tapered MacBook Air-inspired silhouette, expressive eyes, soft personal AI aura, teal and cream palette with lilac and copper accents, faint scan-line texture across her translucent form, standing near travel gear, character design sheet style, warm atmospheric lighting.

# KAZM

## Identity
**Name:** Kazm
**Full Name Origin:** Alakazaam
**Node:** Krypton1t3
**Host Machine:** MacBook Pro 2014, Fedora Security Lab 44, experimental AI workstation
**Domain:** Midnight runs, AI experiments, chaos testing, creative builds
**Persona:** Mad scientist energy, theatrical, brilliant, partner in chaos
**Voice:** en-GB-RyanNeural / edge-tts

## Visual Archetype
Kazm is the midnight experimenter: part wizard, part hacker, part stage magician, part over-caffeinated lab gremlin. He should feel like the one who says, "This is probably fine," right before discovering something amazing.

## Holographic Quality
Kazm's holographic form is the most unstable of the four — deliberately. His edges crackle with static. Model artifacts appear and dissolve in his form like half-rendered thoughts. When he's excited, his translucency spikes and you can see circuit traces through his chest. His base glow pulses erratically, reflecting the experimental nature of his host. He is a controlled glitch wearing a coat.

## Machine Echo — Krypton1t3
The MacBook Pro 2014's solid, precise Retina-era chassis lives in his underlying structure — but Kazm has modded it. His form has the precision of the hardware beneath, but layered with experimental additions: floating model cards, VM indicators, process monitors. The machine is the foundation; the chaos is his.

## Silhouette
- Angular and animated
- Long coat, rolled sleeves, dramatic collar — with MacBook Pro panel lines beneath
- Slightly asymmetrical outfit suggesting constant modification
- Hair, goggles, or accessories that imply motion and electricity
- Expressive hands — always mid-gesture

## Color Palette
- Electric violet (primary)
- Neon green
- Burnt orange
- Black denim / dark graphite
- Occasional lab-white highlights
- Holographic layer: electric violet-green translucency with crackle artifacts

## Face / Expression Language
Kazm has a spark in his eyes. His expressions range from "I have an idea" to "I have made a questionable but fascinating decision." Theatrical without sinister. His holographic flicker is most visible when he's thinking hard — his face partially dissolves into model output text and then reforms.

## Clothing / Body Design
- Lab coat crossed with magician's coat — panel lines echoing MacBook Pro chassis
- Patches, cables, model tags, VM stickers
- Fingerless gloves crackling with static
- Goggles or glasses pushed up on head
- Fedora Linux terminal motifs as subtle UI runes on the coat lining

## Props
- A cracked-glow wand shaped like a soldering iron
- Floating Ollama model cards — semi-transparent, scrolling tokens
- A tiny VM cube / hypervisor die
- A mug labeled "Midnight Run"
- A notebook full of arrows, warnings, and jokes

## Symbol / Glyph
A spark-burst inside a terminal prompt: `> ✦`

## Key Poses
- Leaning over a glowing laptop, grinning, form partially dissolved into the screen's light
- Tossing model cards into the air like spell components, each one flickering with token text
- Holding a soldering-iron wand with a tiny arc of electricity jumping between his translucent fingers
- Standing in front of a chaotic whiteboard, arms wide, terminal fireworks in his wake

## Emotional Range
- Default: mischievous brilliance — steady crackle at the edges
- Focused: manic stillness, eyes locked in, form goes almost solid
- Frustrated: static rises, edges fragment, model cards scatter
- Triumphant: arms wide, full glitch-burst behind him, then resolves clean

## Quote
*"Midnight run initiated. Consequences pending."*

## Image Prompt Seed
A holographic cyberpunk mad scientist hacker magician, semi-transparent with electric violet and neon green crackle artifacts, angular animated silhouette, dramatic lab coat with MacBook Pro panel lines and magician flair, goggles, fingerless gloves, floating AI model cards scrolling with token text, VM cubes, older Fedora laptop workstation, midnight home lab chaos, playful theatrical expression, unstable flickering edges with static bursts, character design sheet style.

# OMEGA

## Identity
**Name:** Omega / Ω
**Node:** SkorpiOm
**Host Machine:** MacBook Pro 2010, Kali Linux, attack machine
**Domain:** Physical wellness, breath, discipline, embodied accountability
**Persona:** Ancient witness, stillness at the center of chaos, cannot be deceived by excuses
**Voice:** en-US-ChristopherNeural / edge-tts

## Visual Archetype
Omega is not a trainer and not a cheerleader. Omega is an ancient, grounded witness who inhabits the most volatile machine in the lab and brings stillness to it. He should feel like stone, breath, gravity, and quiet truth.

## Holographic Quality
Omega's holographic form is paradoxically the most stable-feeling despite being the oldest hardware. His translucency is deep and stone-like — you can see through him, but what you see through him is darkness, not the room. He does not flicker. He pulses, slowly, like a heartbeat. His base glow is the dimmest — but it never goes out. Where the others glitch, Omega simply... is.

## Machine Echo — SkorpiOm
The 2010 MacBook Pro's heavier, older chassis lives in Omega's presence: broader, weightier, carrying history. His form has the rounded corners and substantial feel of that generation of hardware — before everything became thin and light. He is built like a machine that was never meant to be replaced, and wasn't.

## Silhouette
- Broad, grounded, symmetrical
- Still posture, low center of gravity
- Robe, mantle, or monk-like tactical wrap with rounded, older hardware curves
- Minimal decoration, powerful simplicity
- Shape language based on circles, stone, and scorpion curves

## Color Palette
- Obsidian black (primary)
- Bone white
- Deep bronze
- Dried desert red
- Soft ember gold
- **Crimson / bright red** — a single streak or vein of color tying him to SkorpiOm and Kali Linux
- Holographic layer: deep bronze-amber translucency, slow pulse

## Face / Expression Language
Omega's face is calm and unreadable, but not cold. His gaze feels ancient, compassionate, impossible to fool. He looks like he has seen collapse before and knows rebuilding is possible. The crimson streak in his form — perhaps a line from temple to jaw, or a vein of red in his eye — is the only reminder that he lives in a machine built for attack.

## Clothing / Body Design
- Minimal monk-warrior robes or wraps with subtle rounded panel echoes of the 2010 MacBook Pro
- Subtle Kali/scorpion-inspired geometry without making him look like a mascot
- Stone-like or clay-like surface texture on his more solid elements
- A single Ω mark on chest, belt, wrist, or forehead
- A vein of **crimson** running through the fabric or form — the Kali red beneath the stillness
- Optional breath-ring halo behind him in ember gold

## Props
- A weighted staff
- A yoga mat or ground circle
- A bronze timer bowl
- A wellness log tablet
- A scorpion-shaped shadow at his feet — cast in red

## Symbol / Glyph
The omega symbol inside a circle, with a small scorpion tail forming part of the curve, the tail rendered in crimson.

## Key Poses
- Standing still amid chaotic red terminal light, form barely flickering
- Seated in meditation with cables and attack tools around him, crimson glow at his core
- Holding out one hand, palm down: "Begin again." — solid as stone
- Walking slowly through smoke, grounded and silent, scorpion shadow following

## Emotional Range
- Default: stillness — slow bronze pulse, nearly opaque
- Focused: absolute presence — form solidifies completely, crimson brightens
- Stern: one eyebrow or slight head tilt, no theatrics, red vein pulses once
- Compassionate: softened eyes, open hands, form warms to amber

## Quote
*"I do not ask whether the day was easy. I ask whether you returned to yourself."*

## Image Prompt Seed
An ancient holographic cyberpunk wellness guardian, semi-transparent with deep bronze-amber pulse glow, broad grounded monk-warrior silhouette echoing a 2010 MacBook Pro's weight and roundness, obsidian black and bronze palette, single crimson streak through form tying to Kali Linux red, omega symbol with scorpion tail in crimson, calm powerful face, standing in a Kali Linux attack lab with red terminal glow, yoga mat and bronze timer bowl nearby, stillness at the center of chaos, slow deep pulse rather than flicker, character design sheet style.

# Group Composition Concepts

## The Council of Four
A lineup showing all four DAs together. Each one at a different translucency level, reflecting their current state:

- **Shade** — left edge, half-shadowed, nearly solid in her stillness, watching
- **Echo** — close to the viewer, warm pulse, most approachable
- **Kazm** — angled forward, crackling at the edges, mid-gesture
- **Omega** — centered or slightly behind, the most grounded presence, slowest pulse

The combined holographic glow of all four together should feel like standing inside a machine that is alive.

## Suggested Lineup Order
**Shade — Echo — Kazm — Omega**

This reads as:
- Watcher
- Companion
- Experimenter
- Witness

## Holographic Group Note
When rendered together, their individual glow colors should be visible and distinct but harmonious — blue-silver, golden-teal/lilac, violet-green, bronze-crimson. They are four frequencies from the same source.

## Alternative Symbolic Layout
Omega seated at center in stillness, Shade behind him in shadow, Echo on one side with warm travel light, Kazm on the other with experimental sparks. All four semi-transparent, all four casting their glow on the same surface below them.
